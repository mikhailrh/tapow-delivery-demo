## Sourcing & Provenance

Alu-Alu's pitch is genuine farm-to-table — every protein and vegetable category traces back to a sister-company farm. Worth knowing if you're benchmarking, partnering, or pitching against them.

- **Fish (Bayu Aquaculture, Tuaran)** — MyGAP-certified hatchery; voluntary participant in the Aquaculture Residue Monitoring Programme since 2012; reportedly zero detections of any of the 80+ commonly-used fish-farm chemicals in samples to date. Live harvest delivered same-day. Includes giant grouper and rabbitfish (the latter bred in suspended cages on a clean-feed diet including algae).
- **Tiger prawn** — own farm, cement ponds, no antibiotics, no growth hormone, no probiotics. Traditional culture method, disease-prevention-led.
- **Bare-neck (naked-neck / "turken") chicken (Kinarut)** — free-range, corn-fed, no hormones / vaccines / antibiotics, raised up to 120 days before slaughter.
- **Vegetables (Green-Os, Rantau Bakat Sdn Bhd, Kinarut)** — Department of Agriculture organic-certified; harvested early morning, delivered same-day to the restaurant.
- **Eggs & heirloom tomatoes** — same Kinarut organic vegetable farm.

## Notes & oddities

1. **Matrix-priced sections** (Noodles, Noodle Soup, Live Fish, Live Prawn cooking styles) are the trickiest to ingest cleanly — each is effectively an item-template × variant SKU explosion. If you're modelling this for a system, treat the cooking-style code as the parent item and the protein/fish-type as a required modifier group.
2. **Half-chicken pricing on OC07, OC08, OC10** breaks the Small/Medium pattern used elsewhere in the chicken section — flat ½-chicken price, no Medium tier.
3. **Soft Shell Crab base price (RM 45 / 88)** is set once at the section header rather than per-item; CR02 and CR05 add upcharges (+RM 3 and +RM 5 / 4pcs respectively).
4. **OF03 "Shredded Potato with Wasabi Mayo"** is mapped to the Chinese 芥末 (literally "wasabi/mustard"), which doesn't quite cover the dish description — likely an abbreviated menu code rather than a full translation.
5. **N04 "Fried Mee / Mee Hoon"** is mapped to 炒粉面肉类 (literally "stir-fried noodles/meat type") — reads more like a category header than a dish name; could be a layout artifact.
6. **R02 Snapper +RM 2 footnote** is the only à-la-carte upcharge in the Rice section — the default fish isn't specified.
7. **Item codes (R01, N01, F01…)** are present throughout — extremely useful for any structured ingestion (POS sync, ordering app, voice agent). Worth preserving as primary keys.
8. **All prices subject to 6% service tax** — line-item prices shown here are pre-tax; effective customer prices are ×1.06.
9. **Featured live items (lobster, white clams, strombus shells)** don't carry item codes in the source — they're presented as A4 hero pages, suggesting they rotate based on availability. The clams and strombus shells are seasonal-priced.
10. **Several Chinese names contain mixed simplified/traditional characters** (e.g. 雞 in OC06 佛钵宫保雞丁 vs 鸡 elsewhere; 風 in F10 避風塘 vs 风 elsewhere). Preserved verbatim from the menu.
